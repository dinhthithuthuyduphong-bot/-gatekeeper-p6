## What this package deliberately keeps measurable

Source match, dynamic reach, oracle validity, observed regression, statistical
admission and deployment safety are different milestones. A source scan cannot
skip any of the later ones. All new candidates remain unvalidated until the raw
evidence is inspected. No treatment adapter is supplied for Pydantic/Airflow in
this round because their target-specific oracle has not been established.

If all candidates fail eligibility, retain the negative search results and the
selection log. An empty new cohort is a valid outcome of discovery; it is not a
reason to invent a tier-1 oracle or recycle exposed outcomes as prospective.

After the returned artifact, prioritize the candidate with an existing upstream
assertion that distinguishes the exact frozen transform, not the candidate with
the most syntactic matches. Do not tune method parameters based on those outcomes
and report the same sample as a confirmatory evaluation.
