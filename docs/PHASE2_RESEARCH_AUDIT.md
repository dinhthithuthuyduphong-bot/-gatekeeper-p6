## 3. Confidence sequences: additional reporting, not changing decisions

Let `Z_i = loss_with - loss_without` in `[-1,1]`,
`X_i = 1{pass_without and fail_with}` in `{0,1}`. For each study with K
declared cells, family error 0.05 is split over **2K** two-sided
sequences (mean and harm rate). For each sequence and time n:

```
delta = 0.05 / (2*K)
delta_n = delta/(n*(n+1))
radius_n = (upper-lower)*sqrt(log(2/delta_n)/(2*n))
CS_n = [mean_n - radius_n, mean_n + radius_n] intersect [lower,upper]
```

This is a simple Hoeffding + union-bound CS that is valid at arbitrary
stopping times given i.i.d. bounded scope draws. It is *different from*
the frozen e-value-based betting inference and often much more
conservative. Interpretation must remain separate.

Approximate interval examples from the stopped prefixes:

| Cell | n | μ_hat | 95% family-simultaneous μ CS | h_hat | harm CS |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| D1_py_ecc/bool | 110 | -0.773 | [-1.000, -0.224] | 0 | [0, 0.274] |
| D3_speckit/bool | 110 | -0.509 | [-1.000, +0.040] | 0 | [0, 0.274] |
| D5_pycubrid/bool | 300 | -0.110 | [-0.462, +0.242] | 0 | [0, 0.176] |
| E1_pyyaml/bool | 2 | +1.000 | [-1.000, +1.000] | 1 | [0, 1.000] |

The harm CS above do NOT certify `h < eta=0.05` even when frozen
e-based certificates are conclusive. This does not constitute a
contradiction: the procedures have different statistical strength.
The raw trace analysis tool generates all cells, not just this excerpt,
and excludes full-B8-budget continuation past Gatekeeper stopping.

## 4. Study F held-out scoring blockers

**Blocking technical/reproducibility concerns detected in the frozen code:**

- `tools/score_heldout.py` is not included in the `study_f/FREEZE.json`
  `files` mapping, although it determines held-out evaluation.
- `tools/score_heldout.py` has a per-scope `gate()` that requires scope
  decision `ADMIT`. The inspected workflow runs this per target but
  has no explicit final 48-cell e-Holm aggregation. The per-scope
  threshold may be conservative, but that does not substitute for
  auditing the precise reported FWER claim and final policy.
- `k_cells = 48` describes 4 models × 6 targets × 2 scopes, while
  three repeated model repairs per cell are planned. The desired
  family of statistical claims across repetitions must be justified
  before collecting paid observations.
- The frozen protocol heading still says "DRAFT" even though the
  config is `FROZEN`. Correct with an append-only explanatory
  amendment, not by modifying the hashed original.
- In the frozen `tools/run_b9.py`, evidence type is assigned from
  `mode='record'`. Independent provider-usage and cassette/billing
  provenance should be checked before results are presented as
  real paid-model evidence.

**Proposed disposition:** append-only prospective amendment/release
attestation, pinned scorer hash and explicit multiplicity contract,
created and reviewed *before* any paid `record` calls. Do not rewrite
Study F historical freeze or its seed after looking at outcomes.
